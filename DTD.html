DTD: Death TD

DTD: Death TD is a tower defense game in which you must defend your base from waves of zombies using various makeshift weapons.

On this part I was part of a team of programmers, I was in charge of designing and implementing the enemy AI, deciding what kind of enemies we would have, how they would behave and how they would interact with the player. I also worked in the balancing of the game to make sure that the game had the proper ammount of challenge.
