Time Heist

Time Heist is a very simple game, and it was the very first project I worked on during university.

This was a project made for a 48 hour game jam, in which none of the 5 team memebers had ever even touched unity. We had to learn the basics of unity and c# in order to make a simple game. I was in charge of the programming of the game, implementing the core mechanics and making sure that everything worked as intended. The game is a simple fruit ninja style game, in which you have to click the treasure pieces that appear on the screen, while avoiding the black holes that will make you lose the game.
